Research Assistant, Computer Vision

Sep 2025 – Jun 2026

FAB Lab 360, Independent University, Bangladesh · Supervisor: Dr. Md Tarek Habib

  • Designed FishMorphHybridNet, a 7.47M‑parameter CNN–Transformer hybrid for fine‑grained classification of 16 endangered Bangladeshi freshwater fish species, reaching 98.54% test accuracy and 0.9854 macro F1 trained entirely from random initialisation, without ImageNet pre‑training.
  • Established a controlled eight‑architecture benchmark under a single aligned recipe — identical splits, augmentation, optimiser, schedule, precision policy and seed — isolating architecture as the only free variable; the proposed model outperformed every lightweight and heavyweight baseline, including MaxViT‑T, while using 4.07× fewer parameters.
  • Identified shortcut learning in the strongest baseline through a Grad‑CAM audit: MaxViT‑T localised its evidence on segmentation mask borders and uniform background corners rather than on fish anatomy — a failure mode invisible to aggregate accuracy.
  • Used Grad‑CAM comparison to justify the hybrid design over its higher‑accuracy CNN‑only ablation, showing the Transformer encoder produces continuous activation across the full anterior–posterior body axis where the convolutional arm relies on fragmented local texture anchors.
  • Constructed and curated the underlying image corpus — acquisition, cleaning, class balancing and augmentation — for a low‑data regime in which no prior dataset for these species existed.
  • Authored the earlier six‑species study (custom CNN, 8,676 field‑collected images, 97.42% accuracy), published in Springer LNNS (Scopus indexed) and presented as an oral paper at the AIM Conference, Manila (Dec 2025).
